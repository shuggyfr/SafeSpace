# SafeSpace

An interactive blog where people post problems, big or tiny, and people who've lived them give their 2 cents.

This is a clickable prototype: a single `index.html` with sample posts. Anything you add is saved in your browser only (localStorage).

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## What's in it

- Feed with topics and sorting (Fresh, Most relatable, Needs advice)
- Ask: post a problem under your alias
- Aliases: everyone is anonymous but still feels like a real person, with chosen pronouns
- Give your 2 cents (replies), with a "Been through it" mark
- Reactions: "Been there" on posts (the like), "This helped" on 2 cents
- Notifications, e.g. "Tina gave her 2 cents"
- The asker can mark the 2 cents that sorted it
